# The One-Liner Groan-Off — groan-off

> I told my son I'd become a magician. He laughed — until I made his allowance disappear.

The joke sets up a classic dad boast — a father announcing a glamorous new career as a magician — and the son's laugh seems to win the exchange, until the punchline turns the magic trick on the one thing every kid cares about: his allowance. The vanishing act lands because it is exactly the kind of petty, loving power move a dad would pull at the dinner table, and the groan comes from realizing the son laughed one second too soon.
